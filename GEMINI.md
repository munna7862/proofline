# GEMINI.md

The shared rules for this repo are in AGENTS.md (Antigravity reads it automatically in recent versions).
If your Antigravity version does not, treat AGENTS.md as if it were pasted here.

Antigravity specifics:
- Workflows are in `.agent/workflows/` (sprint-start, verify, review, plan-sprint). If your version expects `.agents/`, rename the folder.
- Personas are described in docs/PERSONAS.md. When a workflow says "act as the Architect", follow that persona's section.
- Use the browser agent to look at `.proofline/report/index.html` and `demo` pages after report or agent changes, and attach a screenshot to your walkthrough.
